Questions about packages

Why aren't prices shown?

Every project is scoped to the business. After a short call we send a written quote, so you only pay for what you need.

Can I combine or change a package?

Yes. Packages are starting points. We can add features from a higher tier or remove what you don't need.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. We deliver for clients around the world and work remotely by WhatsApp, email and video call.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes support after launch. After that, we can continue with monthly improvements or fix issues as they come up.
