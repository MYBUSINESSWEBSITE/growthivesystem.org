Molly Samuels

Fashion and school-uniform e-commerce, Nigeria and the UK

The challenge
Two businesses, Mollys (fashion and lifestyle) and M. Samuels (school uniforms), needed one online store serving customers in Nigeria and the United Kingdom.
What we built
A unified storefront with a section for each division, naira and pound pricing from one exchange rate, cart and wishlist, customer accounts, bulk and custom uniform orders, and an admin dashboard with permissioned admin users.
Technology
HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Supabase for data and accounts, GitHub Pages on a custom domain.
Status
Live at mollysamuels.com.
