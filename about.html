About

A founder-led company building practical technology.

Growthive Systems is a technology and business solutions company in Abuja. We build software, business systems and digital growth for organisations in Nigeria and abroad.

From helping businesses look better to helping them work better.

Growthive started with branding, design and social media for small businesses. Again and again, the same thing came up. A business could look great online and still lose money to scattered records, missed bookings and guesswork.

So we started building the systems behind the brand. Growthive Monitor now helps 2,000 people keep proper business records. Growthive Book gives service businesses a simple way to take bookings. Our client work grew too, from websites to a two-market e-commerce platform.

Our mission is simple: design and deliver practical digital solutions that help organisations run better, use their information well and grow through technology.

David Ayogu Abuchi

Founder, Growthive Systems

David leads Growthive's product development and client delivery. He built Growthive Monitor and Growthive Book from the ground up, and leads every client project from scoping to launch.

Alongside Growthive, he works in AI training and evaluation, including data annotation and prompt engineering for international AI teams. That work shapes how Growthive approaches quality, testing and clear documentation.

His degree in psychology, with a focus on organisational psychology, is why Growthive designs systems around how people actually work. He also holds certifications in data analytics, Power BI and AI prompt engineering.
