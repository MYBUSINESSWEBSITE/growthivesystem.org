From helping businesses look better to helping them work better.

Growthive started with branding, design and social media for small businesses. Again and again, the same thing came up. A business could look great online and still lose money to scattered records, missed bookings and guesswork.

So we started building the systems behind the brand. Growthive Monitor now helps 2,000 people keep proper business records, and Growthive Book gives 1,300+ users a simple way to take bookings. Our client work grew too, from websites to a two-market e-commerce platform.

Our mission is simple: design and deliver practical digital solutions that help organisations run better, use their information well and grow through technology.

David Ayogu Abuchi

Founder & Software Engineer, Growthive Systems

“Helping businesses move from scattered efforts to structured, scalable, high-performing systems.”

Growthive Systems was founded by David Ayogu Abuchi, a software engineer and business systems builder with a background in industrial and organisational psychology, focused on helping businesses achieve structured, sustainable growth through technology.

His work sits at the intersection of software engineering and growth-driven strategy, where ideas are transformed into working systems that enable businesses to scale with clarity and consistency. He designs and builds full-stack web applications, from database design and authentication to the interfaces customers use every day.

He built Growthive's own products from the ground up: Growthive Monitor, a business management app with 2,000 users in four languages, and Growthive Book, a booking platform with 1,300+ users. Both run on a modern serverless stack of Supabase, PostgreSQL and JavaScript.

With 5 years of experience across software, branding, digital media and organisational development, he builds systems that are not only visible, but also designed to perform, adapt and expand in evolving markets, for clients around the world.

Alongside Growthive, he works in AI training and evaluation, including data annotation and prompt engineering for international AI teams, and holds certifications in data analytics, Power BI and AI prompt engineering.

Driven by discipline, purpose and a commitment to excellence, David is focused on creating solutions that deliver both immediate impact and sustainable growth. Beyond business, he is committed to building impact through his foundation, supporting growth, learning and opportunities within communities.
