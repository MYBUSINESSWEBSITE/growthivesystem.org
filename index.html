Products we build

We don't only build for clients. We run our own software.

Growthive Monitor and Growthive Book are products we design, engineer and support ourselves. Together they serve more than 3,300 users.

Live and free

Growthive Monitor

A record-keeping app with 2,000 users. Record daily sales, see cash, transfer and POS takings separately, and know your profit without a calculator.

  • Daily sales and expenses with automatic profit
  • Debts you owe and debts owed to you
  • English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo

Know your stock before it runs out

Every item has a low-stock alert. Monitor shows what your stock is worth, what you paid for it and the profit you should expect.

Weekly, monthly and quarterly reports

Revenue against expenses, profit trends and payment methods, with exports to PDF and Excel.

Growthive Book

A booking platform with 1,300+ users. One link for salons, barbers, lash artists and other service businesses. Clients pick a service and an open slot, with no back-and-forth on WhatsApp.

Every booking in one place

Confirm, reschedule or decline bookings, mark no-shows and add walk-ins. Completed bookings can flow into Monitor as income.
