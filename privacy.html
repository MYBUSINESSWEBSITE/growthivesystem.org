Privacy

How this website handles your information.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, and optionally your company, phone number, budget, timeline and message. The form is delivered through Formspree.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and discuss your project. We don't sell your information or add you to marketing lists without asking.

Cookies and browser storage

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell data.

  • Essential storage: we save small settings in your browser, such as your cookie choice and whether you've seen the intro animation. These never leave your device.
  • Embedded videos: our videos are hosted on YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube only sets its cookies if you press play. If you decline optional cookies, videos open on YouTube instead of playing on this page.
  • Third-party files: fonts and animation scripts load from Google Fonts, cdnjs and unpkg, which may see your IP address while delivering those files.

You can change your choice at any time with the link.
